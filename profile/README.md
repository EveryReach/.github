## 中文

### EverReach 🌍

> **让万物通达每一个人** | *Making Everything Accessible to Everyone*

#### 📖 关于我们

EverReach 的使命很简单，但又很大胆：**打破 AI 的象牙塔**。

我们相信：

- 🌉 真正伟大的技术从来不是用来制造鸿沟的，而是用来**搭建桥梁**的
- 💧 AI 应该像水和电一样**自然地融入生活**，而非高高在上的玄学
- 🔑 AI 不应该是少数精英的专属魔杖，而应该成为**每个人的力量**
- ♾️ 万物相通，意味着让 AI **通达每一个人**，而非让极少数人通达万物

我们在构建一个开放、友好、人人都能参与的 AI 生态。无论你是研究者、工程师、学生，还是对 AI 充满好奇的普通人——这里都是属于你的地方。

#### 🌈 如何参与

我们欢迎各种形式的贡献：

- **代码贡献** - 提交 PR 改进项目
- **文档和教程** - 用浅显易懂的方式解释 AI 概念
- **想法和反馈** - 在 Issues 中分享你的观点
- **翻译和本地化** - 让 AI 跨越语言的界限
- **应用案例** - 分享你用 AI 做出的有趣东西


#### 🍿 有趣的事实

- EveryReach 的名字灵感来自于一个简单的想法：让每个人都能 reach AI，AI 也能 reach 每个人

## English

### EverReach 🌍

> **Making Everything Accessible to Everyone** | *让万物通达每一个人*

#### 📖 About Us

EveryReach's mission is simple, yet bold: **Break AI out of its ivory tower**.

We believe:

- 🌉 Truly great technology is never used to create gaps, but to **build bridges**
- 💧 AI should naturally integrate into our lives like water and electricity, not remain a mysticism on high
- 🔑 AI should not be an exclusive wand for a few elites, but a **force for everyone**
- ♾️ The interconnectedness of all things means enabling AI to **reach every person**, not enabling a few to reach everything

We're building an open, friendly, and inclusive AI ecosystem. Whether you're a researcher, engineer, student, or simply curious about AI—this is a place for you.

#### 🌈 How to Contribute

We welcome all kinds of contributions:

- **Code Contributions** - Submit PRs to improve projects
- **Documentation and Tutorials** - Explain AI concepts in accessible ways
- **Ideas and Feedback** - Share your thoughts in Issues
- **Translation and Localization** - Help AI transcend language barriers
- **Use Cases and Examples** - Share the interesting things you've built with AI


#### 🍿 Fun Facts

- The name "EverReach" was inspired by a simple idea: enable everyone to reach AI, and enable AI to reach everyone
- We believe the best code comments are explanations that your grandmother would understand
